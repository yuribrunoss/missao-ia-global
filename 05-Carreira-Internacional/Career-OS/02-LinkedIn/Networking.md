# Networking Mission

Build a high-quality international professional network focused on AI Engineering, Backend Development and AI Automation.

---

## Target Audience

### AI Engineers

Follow professionals building AI products, agents and automation systems.

### Backend Engineers

Follow developers specialized in APIs, databases and scalable architectures.

### Recruiters

Connect with recruiters hiring remote software engineers and AI professionals.

### Startup Founders

Connect with founders building AI startups and SaaS products.

---

## Weekly Networking Routine

### Monday

Connect with AI Engineers.

### Tuesday

Connect with Backend Developers.

### Wednesday

Engage with Recruiters.

### Thursday

Engage with Startup Founders.

### Friday

Publish engineering progress.

### Weekend

Review connections and interact with posts.

---

## Networking Principles

* Quality over quantity.
* Build in public.
* Share engineering progress.
* Engage before asking for opportunities.
* Keep a professional technical profile.

---

## Career Goal

Develop an international network that supports remote opportunities in AI Engineering and Backend Development.
