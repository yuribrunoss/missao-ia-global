# 🧠 Color Meaning

## 🟣 AI Purple

Represents intelligence, innovation and artificial intelligence.

Use for:

- AI.
- Agents.
- Main buttons.
- Hero sections.

Gradient pair:

`#7C3AED → #9333EA`

---

## 🔵 Backend Blue

Represents engineering and software architecture.

Use for:

- Backend.
- FastAPI.
- PostgreSQL.
- APIs.
- Docker.

Gradient pair:

`#2563EB → #1D4ED8`

---

## 🟢 Mission Green

Represents evolution and completed missions.

Use for:

- Success messages.
- XP.
- Completed roadmap.
- Dashboard metrics.

Gradient pair:

`#16A34A → #15803D`

---

## 🟠 Energy Orange

Represents movement and focus.

Use for:

- Current mission.
- Goals.
- Alerts.
- Important information.

Gradient pair:

`#EA580C → #C2410C`

---

## ⚫ Dark Surface

Primary dark background.

Use for:

- GitHub.
- VS Code.
- Dashboards.
- Portfolio.

HEX ` #0F172A`

---

## ⚪ Light Surface

Support background.

Use for PDFs and documentation.

HEX ` #F8FAFC`

# 🎯 Usage Rules

## GitHub

Background: Dark Surface

Accent: AI Purple

Links: Backend Blue

Success: Mission Green

## LinkedIn

Banner: AI Gradient

Icons: White

Highlights: Mission Green

## YUYU AI ERP

Primary: Backend Blue

AI Modules: AI Purple

Notifications: Mission Green

Warnings: Energy Orange

---

# 🚫 Avoid

- Red as primary brand color.
- Yellow backgrounds.
- More than four accent colors on one screen.

Consistency is part of the brand.
