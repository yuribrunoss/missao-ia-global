# 💼 Banner Blueprint — YUYU AI Studio

> **Version:** 1.0.0 — Elite Edition
>
> **Status:** Approved

---

# Executive Summary

This blueprint defines the visual composition of the official LinkedIn banner used across the YUYU AI Studio ecosystem.

The design follows the Brand System and represents the Engineering + Artificial Intelligence identity.

---

# Objectives

The banner must communicate in less than five seconds:

- Who Yuri Bruno is.
- What YUYU AI Studio does.
- Core technologies.
- International positioning.

---

# Visual Hierarchy

1. Name.
2. Professional Role.
3. Mission Statement.
4. Tech Stack.
5. Brand.
