# 👤 Avatar Specification — YUYU AI Studio

> Version 1.0.0 — Elite Edition

## Executive Summary

Official specification for the professional avatar used across YUYU AI Studio.

---

## Purpose

Create one consistent professional portrait for LinkedIn, GitHub, Portfolio and YUYU AI products.

---

## Visual Style

- Dark background.
- Purple and blue lighting.
- Minimalist composition.
- Natural facial appearance.

---

## Clothing

- Black shirt or black t-shirt.
- Clean and professional.

---

## Composition

- Face centered.
- Shoulders visible.
- Neutral posture.
- Looking at the camera.

---

## Export Formats

- PNG
- JPG
- WebP

---

## Platforms

- LinkedIn
- GitHub
- Portfolio
- Documentation

---

## Status

Official Avatar Specification.
